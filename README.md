# Insurance Claims & Policy Analysis | Tableau

## Project Overview
This project analyzes insurance claims, policy premiums, customer characteristics, and risk patterns using interactive Tableau dashboards.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze insurance claims and policy performance.
- Explore customer and risk patterns.
- Visualize key metrics using interactive dashboards.

## Dashboards
1. **Executive Summary** – Overview of insurance policies, premiums, and claims.
2. **Claim & Risk Analysis** – Analysis of claim amounts, severity, and risk factors.
3. **Customer & Policy Insights** – Customer characteristics and policy comparisons.

## Tools Used
- Tableau
- Microsoft Excel
